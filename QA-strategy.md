**Objective**\
Assess the quality and reliability of the core ecommerce customer journeys and identify opportunities to improve automated regression coverage.\
**Scope**\
The assessment focuses on the customer-facing ecommerce experience, including product discovery, product selection, basket management, account functionality and checkout.\
**Approach**\
Testing will combine exploratory testing, risk-based test selection, functional testing, cross-browser testing, accessibility checks and automated regression testing.\
**Priority**\
Higher priority will be given to journeys which directly affect revenue or prevent customers from completing a purchase.
